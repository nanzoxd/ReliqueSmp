Server rules

The baseline expectations for everyone in Relique. Breaking these gets you muted, kicked, or banned depending on severity — staff have final say.

Discord and general

General conduct

  1. Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, or targeted insults — in text, voice, or DMs between members while representing the server.
  2. No spam or self-promo. Don't flood channels, mass-mention people, or advertise other servers/content without staff approval.
  3. Keep it SFW. No NSFW content, links, or usernames/profile pictures anywhere in the server.
  4. Use the right channel. Keep discussion on-topic — general chat for chat, support channels for help requests, etc.
  5. No cheating or exploits. X-ray, hacked clients, duplication glitches, and known bugs are bannable. Report bugs to staff instead of abusing them.
  6. Listen to staff. Moderator and admin decisions are final in the moment — appeal through the proper channel if you disagree.

In-game rules

Combat & PvP

  1. No combat logging. This includes vision logging and chase logging. Combat timer is 1 minute.
  2. No Elytra in combat or vision.
  3. Banned in combat: End Crystals, Respawn Anchors, and bed bombing.
  4. TNT minecarts are allowed only for traps.

Cheats & Exploits

  1. No duping, hacking, cheating, or glitch abuse.
  2. No hacked clients, world maps, seed cracking, or similar tools.
  3. Minimap, Freecam, and FriendGuard mods are not allowed.
  4. Replay is allowed but must not be used unfairly.

World & Gameplay

  1. The End is closed until the End War event.
  2. Do not interfere in ongoing cases.
  3. No loophole abuse.

Spawn rules

  1. No PvP, ambushes, or griefing at spawn.
  2. Do not break or place unnecessary blocks at spawn.
  3. No FriendGuard.
  4. No killing players at spawn who are building.
  5. No fighting inside spawn. This will be strictly enforced.
  6. Do not run into spawn during a fight to save yourself. Doing so will result in punishment.
  7. Do not use /home or any teleport commands while within range of other players, or to escape a fight.

Player safety

  1. New players are protected for their first 30 minutes.
  2. Don't farm naked players at spawn.

Naked player protection

  1. No killing naked players within a 200x200 area of spawn.
  2. If the naked player steals your items or attacks you, you are permitted to kill them.

Teams & alliances

  1. Maximum 10 players per team.
  2. All teams must be registered.
  3. No allied team is allowed.

Temporary alliances

  1. Short-term teaming is allowed only to take down an overpowered player (example: a mace user).
  2. These alliances must end immediately once the threat is gone.
  3. Permanent alliances are not allowed.
  4. Base sharing with temporary allies is not allowed.

Targeting & fairness

  1. Targeting one strong player first in a public fight (including at spawn) is allowed for balance purposes, not favoritism.
  2. Use basic logic and fair judgment during fights.

Enforcement

  1. Abusing the alliance rule to form long-term teams or gain an unfair advantage will result in punishment.
  2. Replay or recording is required as proof. Breaking these rules may result in punishment or removal from the server.

Accounts & IOUs

  1. IOUs require physical proof only.
  2. IOUs with allies or temporary teammates are invalid.

Replay requirement

  1. Replay Mod must be ON at all times.
  2. Replay must not be used unfairly.
  3. No replay means no valid proof.

Important

Replay Mod is not valid hacking proof due to delay. The same applies to Flashback. Replay/Flashback is valid for showing combat logs, vision logs, and similar evidence.

Reports & punishment

  1. Rule breaks must be reported to the owner or an admin.
  2. The reporting player helps choose the punishment from options given by the owner.

Staff enforcement

Enforcement

Warnings & strikes

Most first offenses get a warning. Repeated or severe violations escalate straight to a mute, kick, or ban at staff discretion.

Appeals

Think a punishment was a mistake? Open a ticket in the support channel and a staff member will review it.

The concept

Nine classes, one resource: Strength. Every weapon and the armor slot is bound to its own ability pair — a passive that's always working in the background, and an ultimate you unleash on command. What gates it all is a single stat, capped at 5, that you build up by fighting and lose when you die.

The Strength stat

Every player starts at 3 out of a possible 5. Strength adds flat bonus damage to your hits — roughly two-thirds of a heart per point — and at 0 or below you're actively penalized, dealing reduced damage instead.

1

Fight to build Strength

Passives are live at any Strength ≥ 0 — no threshold needed.

2

Hit max Strength (5)

This is the unlock condition for firing your class's ultimate.

3

Activate with /sability

Or sneak + offhand-click. Ability enters its active duration window, then the 60s class cooldown starts.

4

Die, and pay for it

You drop one Strength point as a physical shard on death — someone else can pick it up.

Why the loop holds together

Passives never turn off

They read your current Strength every tick, so even a player sitting at 0 still gets some class identity — just a weaker one.

Ultimates are a spend, not a switch

You need full Strength (5) to fire one, and doing so locks that class into its cooldown — you can't chain ultimates back to back.

Death is the great equalizer

Losing 1 Strength and dropping a shard on death means the stat is contested — someone else can walk over and pick up what you lost.
